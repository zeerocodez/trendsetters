Questions & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Montessori curriculum, admissions, school hours, and family discounts.

We provide automatic family discounts when you enroll two or more children concurrently. Families with two children enjoy a 10% tuition concession, three children receive a 15% concession, and families with four or more children receive up to 20% discount on subsequent children. Our interactive calculator above lets you preview your exact savings.

Our campus opens daily at 7:00 AM to welcome early arrivals and remains fully staffed with specialized educators and care chaperones until 6:00 PM. This includes our core academic Montessori session, organic hot lunch, afternoon nap/rest period, and our after-school enrichment club with ballet, chess, and guided homework tutoring.

Using structured synthetic phonics and Montessori multi-sensory materials (sandpaper letters, movable alphabets), our pupils typically begin blending sounds into words between age 2.5 and 3. By age 4, the vast majority are reading full storybooks with comprehension and correct pronunciation.

Character education is integral to our vision. Through daily practical life exercises, children are trained in polite speech, dining etiquette, respect for peers and elders, empathy, and active listening. Outstanding students are honored with the prestigious "Magnificent Manners Award" during our annual ceremonies.

Child safety is paramount. Our campus at #3 Udotung Ubo Street features 24/7 CCTV surveillance, perimeter security, gated access control, an authorized-pickup parent verification system, a dedicated first-aid clinic with on-site nurse, and hygienic meal preparation stations.
